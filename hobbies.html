Online game: Chess

Chess is a two player strategy game that I play online on Lichess. Each player controls 16 pieces and tries to checkmate the opponent's king. A short online game takes about 10 minutes and trains planning, patience and quick decision making.

Physical game: Cricket

Cricket is a bat and ball team game played with 11 players on each side. One team bats and tries to score runs while the other bowls and fields. I play it on weekends with my friends, and it keeps me active and teaches teamwork.
